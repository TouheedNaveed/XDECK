ABOUT

Built for People Who Actually Build Things

XDECK exists because control should be simple, accessible, and fair.

MISSION

Our Mission

We believe everyone deserves instant, tactile control over their computer without spending hundreds of dollars on proprietary hardware. XDECK exists to bridge the gap between your phone and your PC, giving you a powerful, customizable control surface that fits in your pocket.

Not everyone needs a $250 Stream Deck. Some people just want a quick way to mute their mic, switch scenes, or launch an app. XDECK gives you that power with the device you already carry everywhere.

OUR STORY

How It Started

XDECK started because I wanted a Stream Deck. The idea of pressing a button and having a shortcut fire instantly felt like the missing piece in my workflow. But then I looked at the options.

Hardware Stream Decks are great, but they cost $150-$250, sit permanently on your desk, and have a fixed number of buttons. Software alternatives either required subscriptions, were bloated with features I didn't need, or simply didn't work well enough to be reliable.

So I built what I actually wanted: an app that turns your phone into a Stream Deck, with no subscription, no account required, and the freedom to customize everything. One purchase, lifetime access, works over WiFi or from anywhere in the world via cloud.
